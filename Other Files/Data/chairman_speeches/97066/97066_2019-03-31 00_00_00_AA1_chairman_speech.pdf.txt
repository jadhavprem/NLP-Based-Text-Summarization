would use their immense managerial 

and innovative capabilities to serve the

shaped ITC’s vision not only to be an

and inclusive. Today, this commitment 

is manifest in ITC’s growing presence 

across all the sectors of the economy - 

agriculture, manufacturing and services

synergise competitive growth with 

large scale societal value creation and 

environmental stewardship. Recognising 

his outstanding contribution to the nation,

Bhushan, one of the highest civilian 

honours, by the Government of India. 

Mr Deveshwar indeed leaves behind a 

rich legacy, and one that will continue to 

inspire us in the years ahead.
