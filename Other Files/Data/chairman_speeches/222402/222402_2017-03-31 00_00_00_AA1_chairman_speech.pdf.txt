

























12 | Shree Cement Limited   Annual Report 2016-17 | 13

Chairman’s
Words

From a humble start decades ago, Shree has today 

grown to a well-earned position of leadership. The 

journey has been eventful and taken us through 

ups and downs. We have reached where we are 

today; because we had a vision and despite twists 

and turns, we chose to keep moving; keep 

evolving and not getting set in any fixed form! 

‘The Form of No Form’, is reflected in our approach 

of dynamism. What is successful today may not 

guarantee our success tomorrow! We have to 

keep on improving ourselves with times and break 

current benchmarks, even if they were set by us in 

the first place.  
Shri B. G. Bangur
Chairman

The growth journey ahead will be 

eventful and I am sure we all at Shree 

shall continue to innovate our way to 

attain collective success by creating 

value from our evolving approach of 

‘The Form of No Form’!

Looking back, I see Shree having reached its 

current position because we all collectively chose 

to evolve and adapt. The growth journey ahead will 

be eventful and I am sure we all at Shree shall 

continue to innovate our way to attain collective 

success by creating value from our evolving 

approach of ‘The Form of No Form’!

VIEWS AND PERSPECTIVES
